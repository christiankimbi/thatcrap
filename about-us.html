Who Are We

The Madhibha eHub identified that the South African Youth is brimming with untapped potential. These Youth are grappling with an alarming youth unemployment rate and a profound skills deficit. The existing education and employment systems have inadequately equipped a substantial portion of the Youth and have rendered them unemployable. This has not only exacerbated economic disparities but also constrained their capacity to contribute meaningfully to society. Closing this chasm is no longer a matter of personal development but has evolved into a pressing strategic necessity for the nation’s social cohesion, growth and resilience.

The Madhibha eHub is an ecosystem which will grow the economic participation among households and communities that have been excluded from the South African economy. The Madhibha eHub looks to act as a bridge between the disenfranchised, more specifically the Youth, unemployed population (youth and others) and the 2nd economy, and linking them to the 1st economy, the economy and the government. The eHub will focus on people, skills development, and sustainable economic opportunities.

purpose & direction

Our Mission

Madhibha eHub is committed to fostering a sustainable ecosystem that empowers and uplifts economically marginalised households and communities in South Africa. Our mission is to cultivate inclusive economic participation by applying sound business principles, driving growth, and creating opportunities for those traditionally underserved by the mainstream economy. Through innovative initiatives and collaborative partnerships, we strive to build a brighter, more prosperous future for all, leaving no one behind.

the road ahead

Our Vision

Madhibha eHub’s vision is to create a society where economic upliftment is inclusive, and individuals are equipped to take ownership of their own destinies. By nurturing a dynamic ecosystem of learning, entrepreneurship, and community-driven initiatives, we aim to drive sustainable economic growth while fostering a sense of pride and ownership among all South Africans.
