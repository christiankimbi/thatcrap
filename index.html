How and why it all began...

Our origin story

The Madhibha eHub team identified that the South African Youth is brimming with untapped potential. These Youth are grappling with an alarming youth unemployment rate and a profound skills deficit.

The existing education and employment systems have inadequately equipped a substantial portion of the Youth and have rendered them unemployable. This has not only exacerbated economic disparities but also constrained their capacity to contribute meaningfully to society.

Closing this chasm is no longer a matter of personal development but has evolved into a pressing strategic necessity for the nation’s social cohesion, growth and resilience.
